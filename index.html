Our Business Model

Ukambani Honey Hub operates as a marketplace platform connecting verified honey farmers directly with customers across Kenya and beyond. We ensure quality, authenticity, and fair compensation for our farming community.

💰

Commission on Sales

We charge a 15% commission on each honey product sold through our platform. This covers payment processing, marketing, and platform maintenance while ensuring farmers receive 85% of the sale price.

👨‍🌾

Vendor Subscription Plans

Basic Plan (Free): Up to 10 product listings
Premium Plan (KES 2,000/month): Unlimited listings, featured placement
Enterprise Plan (KES 5,000/month): All Premium features plus dedicated support

📦

Logistics & Delivery Fees

Standard delivery fee of KES 200-500 depending on location (Nairobi: KES 200, Other cities: KES 350, Remote areas: KES 500). We partner with reliable courier services to ensure safe delivery.

🎯

Featured Listings

Farmers can promote their products through featured listings at KES 500 per product per week. Featured products appear at the top of search results and on the homepage.

📢

Brand Partnerships

We collaborate with hotels, restaurants, and organic food stores for bulk orders. Partnership deals include volume discounts and custom packaging options.

🎓

Training & Consultation

Beekeeping training workshops (KES 5,000 per farmer) and consultation services (KES 10,000 per session) to help improve production quality.

Our Value Proposition

For Farmers: Access to wider markets, fair pricing, training resources, and direct customer relationships without middlemen exploitation
For Customers: Guaranteed authentic honey, traceability to source, supporting local communities, and competitive pricing
For Community: Economic empowerment of Ukambani region, preservation of traditional beekeeping, and environmental conservation
